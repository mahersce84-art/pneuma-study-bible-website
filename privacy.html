Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

Pneuma Study Bible is a free Android application designed for personal Bible study. This policy explains how personal study content and optional Google Drive backup access are handled.

Personal Bible study data

The app lets you create notes, journal entries, bookmarks, highlights, reading history, and preferences. These study records are stored on your device and can be included in backup files that you choose to create.

Optional Google Drive backups

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests permission to access files it creates or that you explicitly open for use with the app, using Google's drive.file permission. It uses this access to create, list, download, rename, share, and delete Bible study backup files in your Google Drive account. Backups can include your personal notes, journal content and images, bookmarks, highlights, reading history, and settings.

Google Drive access is optional. You can use local Bible study and local backup features without connecting Google Drive. You can disconnect your Google account from the app and manage or delete your backup files through Google Drive. Disconnecting does not automatically delete files already saved to Drive.

Google account information

When you connect Google Drive, the app can display basic account information, such as your Google account name and email address, to identify the connected account. Google handles account authentication and authorization. Google Drive files are stored in your Google account and are subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Pneuma Study Bible has no advertisements, no analytics service, and no user-tracking features. We do not sell your personal Bible study content or Google account information to advertisers.

Data sharing and control

Your personal study content is not publicly shared by the app as part of the backup process. You control when to create and import backups and whether to use Google Drive. If you choose to share a backup file using your device's share feature, the recipient and sharing service may receive its contents.

Security

The app uses Google's authorization mechanisms and requests limited Drive file access. No online storage or transfer method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device, your Google account, and any exported backup files.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
